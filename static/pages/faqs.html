Where do I find...?
Use the Food and Artist main menu buttons to search by vendor name, vendor type, products, specialties or interests. Type can click, type or even speak to find information and location of 400 vendors! You can even bookmark your favorites to find them again later!
Is that really the Purple Painted Lady house?
Yes it is. It is at the center of the main vendor area. Please feel free to look around the outside. It is a private residence however and is not open to the public of festival guests. Please be respectful of it.
When will the Witchy Women perform?
Use the Music main menu for a complete listing of a bands, with times, locations and background info.
How do I get to The Purple Painted Lady Store?
The store is located at 2620 Route 31 in Palmyra, NY. About 2.5 miles north of the festival.
Where can I get a Purple Painted Lady festival shirt?
Search for Purple Painted Lady booth using the Artist menu button. They are located at Purple-1 behind the main house.
How do I install the Love the Purple App?
Tap the install icon in the upper right corner.

If you are on Android you just need to confirm the install and then look for the Purple Painted Lady Festival icon in your app menu to start.

If you are on Apple, follow the instruction popup, add the app to your home screen and then look for the Purple Painted Lady Festival icon in your app menu to start.
Is the Love the Purple App free?
The Love the Purple App is free and also completely ad-free. There is no cost to install and use the app. Use the app to explore everything the Purple Painted Lady Festival has to offer.
Do I have to install the app or can I just use it through my browser?
You do not have to install the app. However, some features will work much better if you install it. We recommend at least bookmarking through a browser. You can remove the app at any time.
How do I remove the Love the Purple App?
Tap and hold the festival icon and drag it to the uninstall bar on your phone screen just like any other app.
I have questions about the Love the Purple App. Where can I get help?
Visit the Festival Information Booth at the main gate for assistance. Please also complete the survey in the app to provide any feedback you have. We love ideas and input from our visitors.
Are there ATMs on the festival?
Yes. ATMs are available at multiple locations throughout the festival. Please visit the Map page in this app for the exact ATM locations.
Can I get a refund on my entrance donation?
All donations are final. Refunds are not available except in limited circumstances. Please visit the Information Booth at the main gate if you have a serious problem.
What happens if it rains?
The festival is open rain or shine. Some events may be delayed or rescheduled due to weather or cancelled due to extreme conditions. Please listen for announcements.
What should I do in a weather emergency?
Follow announcements over the festival loudspeakers and directions from staff.
Is smoking allowed?
No smoking of vaping is allowed anywhere at the festival. This is private property. Please respect the wishes of the owners.
Are pets allowed?
No pets are allowed at the festival. Registered service animals are permitted, but must be registered at the Information Booth.
Where is First Aid located?
First Aid is available on-site during all festival hours near the Main Gate. See the First Aid or Map sections of this app for location details.
Where are restrooms located?
Restrooms are available throughout the festival. See the Facilities or Map sections of this app for locations.
Is there a Lost & Found?
Yes. Lost and Found is available at the Information Booth near the Main Gate. If you lose an item please report it as soon as possible.
What if someone is causing a disturbance?
Please notify festival staff or a uniformed security officer immediately. Disruptive behavior will not be tolerated and may result in removal from the festival.
Are outside food or drinks allowed?
Outside food and beverages are not permitted, except for medical or dietary restriction needs. Visit the Food Truck area or many of the booths for a wide range of food and drink options.
Are strollers and wagons allowed?
Yes. Strollers and wagons are allowed, but please be mindful of other visitors in crowded areas.
Can I walk the festival with alcohol?
Yes, you may if you are of legal age and with a proof of age wrist strap on at all times. You may not leave the festival with alcohol.
Is parking available?
Yes. Parking is available on the festival for visitors and vendors. See the Parking feature on the main menu for complete instructions. Please follow posted signs and staff directions when arriving. Handicap parking is also available.
Do vendors accept credit cards?
Many vendors accept credit/debit cards, but not all. It’s recommended to bring some cash. There are also several ATMs at the Purple Painted Lady Festival.
How can I become a vendor or exhibitor?
Stop by the Information Booth for details if you are a food vendor or artist interested in selling your hand-made products.
Can I become a volunteer?
Yes! We would love the help. Stop by the Information Booth for details.
Is WiFi available?
There is no public Wi-Fi at the festival. Verizon cell service is excellent at the festival.
Are bags allowed?
Yes, but all bags may be subject to inspection for safety.
