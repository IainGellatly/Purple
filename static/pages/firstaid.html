For life-threatening emergencies call 911 immediately

For all other medical needs and first aid assistance, our on-site First Aid Station is open during normal festival hours and is staffed by trained medical professionals.

Please note medical personnel cannot administer medications.

The First Aid Station is located near the Main Gate. Check the Map for the exact location.

If you are unable to reach the First Aid Station, please notify the nearest Festival Staff Member immediately. They will assist you in obtaining medical help.
