How can I become a vendor?

Send a text or email to Trish at 585-750-6056 or tricia@thepurplepaintedlady.com with these exact details:

  • Exact title: "APPLYING FOR THE 2027 TPPL FESTIVAL"
  • Your full name
  • Your business name
  • Exactly two photos of your handmade items/work
  • One photo of your booth setup from a previous event
  • A one-sentence elevator description about what you make
  • A link to your active social media page

Trish is the only person who accepts artists. Payments only go directly through The Purple Painted Lady store checkout. Do not send money via Venmo or other cash apps.

The festival is very selective and gives priority to active artisans with an established social media presence.
