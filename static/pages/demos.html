Expert Presenters

Sally Griswold
Sally is the co-founder and creative force behind Iron Orchid Designs (IOD), a popular DIY lifestyle and decor brand. Alongside her sister Josie Celio, Sally grew up with a passion for vintage items and crafting, eventually launching IOD to create innovative stamps, molds, transfers, and paint inlays. The Purple Painted Lady is grateful to have been an IOD Stockist for at least 10 years now and we love being able to be your IOD supplier. And just wait til you meet Sally...She is as sweet as she is in her videos and radiates positivity and creativity!
Debi Beard
Debi is a popular DIY creator, furniture flipper, and the creator of an all natural, clay-and-chalk-based paint line called DIY Paint that The Purple Painted Lady is a proud retailer of. She shares upcycling tutorials, thrift store makeovers, and crafting tips primarily on YouTube and social media. Trish met Debi at an IOD Stockist event back in 2021 and was incredibly impressed with her generosity of kindness, love and sharing supportive business ideas because she sincerely wants her retailers to be successful. It was at this event, that Trish 100% decided to become part of the DIY community.
Johnathon Mark Mendez
Johnathon is a prominent British furniture artist, photographer, and creative educator widely recognized for his expertise in decorative paint finishes. Based in Lincolnshire, England, he transitioned from a career as a part-time hairdresser to an internationally sought after furniture transformation specialist. Trish met Jonathon at an IOD Stockist event back in 2021 and have been good friends since!
